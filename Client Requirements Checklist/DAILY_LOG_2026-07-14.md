Problem(s) Encountered During the Shift:

1. Found 21 products (mostly chimneys and glass, added mid-June) were showing a literal "$0.00" price instead of a real one — and worse, those items could actually be added to cart and checked out for free, since nothing was stopping a $0 item from going all the way through checkout.

Solution(s) Done for the Problem(s) Encountered:

2. Added an "Inquire about this item" button on every product page, so a customer can send a question about a specific product even if it's not ready to buy yet — reuses the same contact system already in place, so replies show up in the same inbox as everything else.
3. Added a filter that skips known bots/crawlers before they ever get counted in the site's real traffic numbers, and started saving enough detail on remaining visits to double-check an unusual one later if needed.
