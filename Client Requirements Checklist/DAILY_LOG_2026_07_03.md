## What Got Done Today

### 1. Fixed the "Red Error" in Google Search Console
Earlier this week, Google flagged a critical error on the website's homepage. It was throwing a red warning because the homepage's hidden data code was telling Google that generic product categories (like "Oil Lamp Shades" and "Enamel Signs") were actual products for sale—but without a price attached.

We tracked down the hidden code responsible for this, removed the offending lines, and deployed the fix. 
- **Current Status:** Google has officially re-crawled the homepage today and confirmed it is error-free. The 5 critical red flags have been permanently cleared, and the homepage is fully validated.

### 2. Google Merchant Center Products Are Approved
The 22 products we submitted to Google Merchant Center earlier this week are now moving out of the "Under review" phase. As of today, **17 of them are fully approved**.

- **Wait time:** Even though they are approved, it takes Google an extra 24 to 48 hours to actually push these product tiles live onto the public Google Shopping search results. Customers should start seeing them at the top of search results by tomorrow or the day after.

### 3. Bing Webmaster Tools Fully Configured
We finished setting up the website on Bing's search engine counterpart. We submitted the site's sitemap (a map of all the pages) and manually submitted the direct URLs of our top 10 approved products to Bing.

- **Current Status:** Bing has confirmed that the earlier "Meta description too long" error is fixed. Bing's index now has the correct description, and they have added our specific product pages to their crawl queue.

### 4. Replaced the Vercel Generic Icon with the Real Logo
We fixed the generic Vercel placeholder logo (the white triangle) that was showing up in browser tabs.

We updated the website's codebase with the custom `icon.png` logo file, removed the old default `.ico` file, and deployed the changes. The new Acme Vintage Supply logo is now permanently showing on live browser tabs.

### 5. Added Google Customer Reviews to the Thank-You Page
We integrated the "Google Customer Reviews" opt-in feature. This means that when a real customer finishes a purchase and lands on the "Thank you for your order" page, a small popup will appear asking them to rate their experience.

This is a massive win for long-term SEO because positive reviews directly influence how high the store ranks in Google search results. We safely added this to the Shopify checkout backend without touching any of the main website code, ensuring the checkout process remains 100% stable and secure.
