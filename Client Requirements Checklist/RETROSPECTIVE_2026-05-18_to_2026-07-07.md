## The Four Systems

### 1. Acme Vintage Supply — E-Commerce Storefront
Live at acmevintagesupply.com. Full customer-facing catalog, cart, and checkout, including:
- Color variant system (admin toggle, productSet, storefront swatches, cart guards)
- Multi-colour cart (opt-in multi-select PDP, grouped cart display, crate page)
- CSV bulk import, shipping weights, inline price editing
- Product reviews system on Supabase, with admin moderation and status control
- Back-in-stock notifications, order tracking, Communications Hub (waitlist, inbox, bench notes)
- Multi-currency support (CAD/USD/EUR/GBP) with auto-detect via Shopify Markets
- Google Merchant Center integration
- SEO pass: FAQPage JSON-LD, BreadcrumbList on PDPs, robots/sitemap fixes
- Full mobile/tablet responsive pass, custom branded 404

### 2. Admin Dashboard
Built alongside the storefront, not bolted on after:
- Analytics page (revenue, conversion funnel, device breakdown, top products) wired to real data
- Admin activity log
- Promotional Email System: template selector, product picker, 3 custom builders (Bench Notes, New Arrivals, Seasonal Sale), preview modal, template library
- Security hardening: OTP two-factor login, bcrypt password hashing, rate limiting, hardened HTTP headers
- Order fulfillment tracking, shipping label / invoice printing

### 3. Acme Sign — Signage Company Website
A second, independent site built from scratch and deployed to acme-sign.vercel.app:
- Gallery-heavy rebuild covering 236 real job photos, filterable by category
- Dedicated LED Signs and Vehicle Wraps pages, standalone Contact page
- Already shown to and reviewed by the client

### 4. Frasco Industrial — Industrial Services Landing Page
A third independent rebuild, live at frasco-sage.vercel.app — built in a single day.

## What Made This Harder Than "4 Projects"

None of this happened in a vacuum. Built in parallel with the four systems themselves:
- **Security**: 2FA, bcrypt, rate limiting, CSP/security headers — implemented and tested live, not deferred
- **Authentication**: full admin login flow, hardened across 6 separate fixes after initial ship
- **Performance & caching**: CMS force-dynamic fixes, Vercel build fixes, cache-cascade debugging
- **Analytics & tracking**: Google Analytics, Meta pixel, post-checkout tracking snippets
- **Payments**: Shopify Payments activation, bank linkage, end-to-end checkout verification across multiple test passes
- **Merchant integrations**: Google Merchant Center product feed, multi-currency Markets setup

No formal project timeline was followed — priorities were re-ordered continuously based on what was actually blocking, what the client needed next, and what broke.
