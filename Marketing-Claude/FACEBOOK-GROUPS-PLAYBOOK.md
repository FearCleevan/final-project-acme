# Facebook Groups Playbook — Acme Vintage Supply
**For:** Scott (posting manually, from his personal Facebook profile)
**Date:** July 6, 2026

This is a plain-English guide for posting Acme Vintage Supply products in the 27 Facebook groups already gathered. No marketing background needed — just follow the steps for whichever type of group you're in.

---

## The One Rule That Matters Most

**Most groups don't want to see a link or an ad. They want to see a person sharing something interesting.**

Post as **yourself** (your personal profile), not as the "Acme Vintage Supply" Page, unless a group specifically says Pages are welcome. If you're not sure, assume "personal profile only, no direct link" — it's the safest default and works in every group on the list.

---

## Step 1: Before You Post in Any New Group — 60-Second Checklist

Do this once, the first time you join a group:

- [ ] Read the **"Group Rules"** (tap the group name → About → Rules, or look for a pinned post). Most groups list this clearly.
- [ ] Check if there's a **specific day or thread for selling/self-promo** (e.g. "Sell it Saturday", a pinned "For Sale" thread). If so, only post there.
- [ ] Check if the rules say **"no links"** or **"no external websites."** If it doesn't say either way, leave the link out anyway and see the workarounds below.
- [ ] Introduce yourself first if the group is small/tight-knit (a lot of antique collector groups are chatty communities, not just marketplaces) — comment on 2-3 other posts before posting your own.
- [ ] Don't post the exact same message in 5 groups back-to-back on the same day. Space it out — Facebook and group admins both flag that as spam.

If a group breaks any of its own rules against you (e.g. removes your post with no explanation), don't argue — just move to the next group. There are 27 on the list; you don't need all of them to work.

## Step 2: The Two Group Types — and How to Post Differently in Each

Your 27 groups fall into two buckets. Treat them differently.

### Type A — Antique & Vintage Collector Groups
*(e.g. Canada Antiques and Collectibles, Vintage Lamp Collectors, Edmonton Area Antiques & Vintage Items, Lethbridge Antique and Vintage)*

These are hobbyist communities. People here love the **story and craftsmanship**, not a sales pitch. They're your best audience — genuinely interested in oil lamps and vintage signs already.

**How to post:**
- Post a **great photo** of one specific piece (a chimney, a shade, a sign) — close-up, good lighting.
- Tell a small story: what it is, roughly how old, an interesting detail. Collectors love specifics (era, maker, condition).
- Only mention where to get it **if directly asked in the comments**, or if the group explicitly allows it — reply with "I've got more like this if you're curious, feel free to DM me."
- Posting frequency: **1-2 posts per week per group, max.** These are communities you want to be a known, trusted face in — not a shop window.

**Example post (Type A):**
> Picked this one up recently — a duplex oval chimney, heat-resistant glass, fits the classic English double-wick burners. Something about the shape always gets me, this style barely changed in a century. Anyone else collect duplex burners specifically, or is it just me? 😄

Notice: no link, no price, no "shop now." Just genuine sharing that invites conversation. If someone asks "where did you get this / is it for sale" — that's your opening to reply personally.

---

### Type B — Garage Sale / Marketplace Groups
*(e.g. Canadian HO Yard Sale, Winnipeg Garage Sale Map, Virtual Garage Sale Victoria BC, Elk Valley Garage Sale, Tacoma Online Yard Sale)*

These groups exist specifically **for selling things**, so a straightforward listing is expected and welcome — but they usually still don't want an outside link, since that takes people off Facebook (which Facebook itself down-ranks, and some admins ban for the same reason).

**How to post:**
- Photo + price + short description, like a garage sale listing: item, condition, price, "pickup or shipping."
- Use **"Message me"** or **"Comment below"** instead of a link. If you want to mention the website, say something like *"more available on my site, just ask and I'll send the link"* rather than pasting the URL directly — some groups will allow this in a comment reply even if not in the main post.
- These groups move fast — postable more often than collector groups. **2-3 posts per week per group** is fine, since it's expected marketplace behavior.

**Example post (Type B):**
> FOR SALE — Vintage oil lamp glass shades, various styles ($15-$40 each). Great condition, ready to ship or local pickup in Dartmouth NS. Comment or message if interested, happy to send more photos!

## Step 3: What To Do When a Group Won't Allow Pages or Links

This happens a lot — it's not a sign you're doing anything wrong. A few workarounds, roughly in order of how well they tend to work:

1. **Join and post as yourself**, not the business Page (covers ~80% of the "no Pages" restriction).
2. **Drop the link from the post** — mention it only if someone asks in the comments, or say "DM me and I'll send you the link."
3. **Use a shortened, non-promotional-looking way to refer to the site** in comments only — e.g. "search Acme Vintage Supply" rather than pasting the full URL.
4. **Ask the admin directly** — a polite DM like *"Hi, I sell vintage oil lamp parts and signs, is it okay to post photos/listings here, and are links allowed?"* takes 30 seconds and avoids getting your post deleted or getting banned from the group.
5. If a group flatly won't allow any promotion at all (some pure "chat" groups are like this) — just don't force it. Interact normally, and skip that group for selling.

## Step 4: Simple Weekly Rhythm (No Scheduling Tools Needed)

You don't need anything fancy — just spread activity across the week so it doesn't look automated:

| Day | What to do |
|---|---|
| Mon | Post in 2-3 Type A (collector) groups |
| Wed | Post in 2-3 Type B (garage sale) groups |
| Fri | Check comments/messages from earlier in the week, reply to everyone |
| Weekend | Casual — like/comment on other people's posts in your groups (keeps you visible without posting) |

That's roughly 8-12 posts a week across all 27 groups, which is enough to build a presence without looking spammy in any single group.

## What Not to Do

- Don't post the identical copy-pasted message in all 27 groups in one sitting.
